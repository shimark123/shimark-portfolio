About Me

I'm Shimark Ahmed, an IT Project Manager with over two years of hands-on experience delivering Agile projects across fintech, municipal technology, ArchViz/software, and real estate platforms. I started my career as a Business Analyst, which gave me a strong grounding in requirements, process, and client needs before I moved into full project delivery.

My work spans the full project lifecycle — from scoping and planning through sprint execution, stakeholder coordination, RAID log management, and UAT sign-off — with a track record of zero critical post-release incidents. That BA foundation means I'm just as comfortable owning the client relationship as I am owning the delivery plan — spotting where an account can grow, not just keeping it on schedule.

I've led Agile ceremonies for distributed teams, coordinated ERP/CRM integration workstreams, and currently manage delivery across ArchViz and software departments at Atlas Labs from Colombo, Sri Lanka, including budgeting and invoicing. I hold a Google Project Management certification, an IBM Business Analysis certification, am working toward PSM1 (Professional Scrum Master I), and have a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science from Staffordshire University, UK.

I'm currently open to part-time Project Management work, fully remote from anywhere, alongside my role at Atlas Labs — including hybrid roles that blend delivery with client/account management and business development. I thrive in cross-functional environments where clear communication, structured documentation, and proactive stakeholder management make the difference between a project that ships and one that stalls.
